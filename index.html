How it works

A simple loop. Fees come in, hats come home.

1
Trade

Every $CLLCTR trade pays a fee. Fees accrue automatically in the fee wallet.

2
Split

Fees are divided 60 / 20 / 20 — hats, liquidity, operations. The split never changes.

3
Convert

The hat share is converted to Robux through official channels.

4
Acquire

Cheapest Steampunk Robin Hood listing gets bought. The purchase lands in the ledger, the hat lands in the vault.

Steampunk Robin Hood

A limited ROBLOX hat from 2013 — 10,000 made, sold for 10 Robux — and the sole target of this strategy. Limited supply, thin listings, deep nostalgia. We buy the cheapest copy on the market, then the next one. We hold.
